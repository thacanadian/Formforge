# FormForge Community Standards — Review Copy

Community features are for workout accountability, not anonymous public posting. Users must accept these standards before creating community content.

## Prohibited content and conduct

- Harassment, bullying, hate, dehumanizing attacks, or discriminatory slurs.
- Sexual exploitation, sexual content involving minors, non-consensual sexual content, or sexual solicitation.
- Credible threats, instructions to harm another person, encouragement of self-harm, or glorification of dangerous conduct.
- Impersonation, fraud, spam, scams, malware, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Sharing another person’s private, identifying, health, or intimate information without permission.
- Illegal content, copyright infringement, or use of community tools to sell regulated products.
- Medical diagnosis or dangerous advice presented as professional care.
- False, retaliatory, or abusive reporting.

## Moderation

FormForge can automatically reject obvious abusive, threatening, hateful, sexual, or spam text. Users can report content and users, and block other users. Moderators can dismiss reports, remove content, warn users, suspend community access, or ban community access. Private fitness functions can remain available during a community restriction.

## Emergencies

FormForge is not an emergency service. Immediate danger should be reported to local emergency services rather than relying on an in-app report.
