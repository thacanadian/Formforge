# FormForge Health and AI Notice — Review Copy

## Not medical advice

FormForge is a fitness and education tool. It does not diagnose injuries, prescribe treatment, replace a clinician, or provide emergency assistance. AI can misunderstand context or generate incorrect information.

## Safe use

- Consult a qualified professional before a new program when you have a medical condition, are pregnant, take medication affecting exercise, or have unexplained symptoms.
- Stop exercising for severe, sudden, sharp, or worsening symptoms.
- Do not treat calorie, body-fat, recovery, wearable, or AI estimates as clinical measurements.
- Do not delay professional care because of an app response.

## AI transparency

When a user opens AI Coach or FormForge Agent, the user is interacting with an artificial-intelligence system. Offline responses use bundled rules and reference modules. Online responses use a configured third-party model and may send relevant profile and fitness context to that provider. Sources can be displayed when available, but a citation does not guarantee correctness.

## Creator profiles

Editorial profiles are independent summaries. They are not endorsements, exact voice replicas, official programs, or evidence that a creator reviewed a response.
