# FormForge Subscription Terms — Review Copy

## Price and value

The planned standard subscription is **$20.00 per month**, localized where available. The actual purchase screen must prominently show the exact amount charged, billing period, included features, renewal terms, and any trial before confirmation.

## Automatic renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through Apple App Store or Google Play settings before the next renewal. The store account used to subscribe is charged. Store tax, currency, grace-period, refund, and family-sharing rules may apply.

## Cancellation

Cancellation stops future renewal and access ordinarily continues through the paid period. Deleting the app or a FormForge account does not by itself cancel an Apple or Google subscription. Account settings must include a clear link to manage the subscription.

## Trials and promotions

Any trial length and post-trial price must be disclosed before purchase. Unless cancelled before the trial ends, the subscription converts to a paid renewal as shown by the store.

## Refunds

Apple and Google purchases are refunded through the applicable store. FormForge does not promise refunds outside store rules. Mandatory consumer rights still apply.

## Changes

Price changes must follow store-required notice and consent processes. Material reductions in recurring value should be disclosed.
