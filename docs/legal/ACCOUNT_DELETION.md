# FormForge Account and Data Deletion — Review Copy

## In-app deletion

A signed-in user opens **Security**, selects **Delete my account**, enters the current password, and types `DELETE`. Standard-user deletion removes the active account and signs out all sessions.

## Web deletion

The public `/account-deletion` page allows a user to delete the account with email, password, and confirmation. Users who cannot authenticate should contact `[SUPPORT EMAIL]` for an identity-verification process.

## Active data removed

Profile, workouts, nutrition, progress, health imports, AI chat, uploaded photos, community content, sessions, and preferences are removed from the live service where reasonably possible.

## Limited retention

Limited tax, transaction, security, fraud-prevention, moderation, legal, and backup records may be retained when reasonably necessary. By default, local encrypted backups retain the 14 newest copies plus one weekly copy for up to eight weeks. Separately copied or downloaded backups require manual secure deletion.

## Store subscriptions

Deleting FormForge data does not cancel an Apple or Google subscription. Users must cancel through the store subscription-management screen.

## Administrator accounts

The sole active administrator cannot self-delete. Administration must be transferred or the service must be closed through a documented shutdown process.
