## Data collected

- Account and profile data, including name, email, password hash, age confirmation, settings, and subscription status.
- Fitness data, including goals, measurements, workouts, exercise performance, nutrition, habits, check-ins, recovery, pain flags, and progress.
- Optional sensitive data, including progress photos, heart-rate or wearable imports, body-fat information, and user notes.
- AI data, including prompts, responses, source links, usage totals, and relevant fitness context used to answer requests.
- Community data, including nudges, shared workouts, reports, blocks, and moderation records.

- If paid store access is enabled, billing status and transaction identifiers supplied by Apple or Google. FormForge should not store full card numbers.

## Uses

Data is used to provide and personalize the service, sync records, operate AI and community features, secure accounts, prevent abuse, fulfill purchases, respond to support and deletion requests, comply with law, and improve reliability.

FormForge does not sell personal information and must not use health or fitness data for targeted advertising.

## Sharing and processors

Data may be processed by hosting providers such as Render; an AI provider such as OpenAI when online AI is enabled and requested; Apple or Google for store billing; and public-data providers such as Open Food Facts for searches. Data may also be disclosed when legally required, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transfer with appropriate safeguards. Community content is visible only to users permitted by the feature.

## AI choices

Offline coaching does not send prompts to an external model. Online AI can send the user prompt and relevant fitness context to the configured provider. Users should not submit secrets or another person’s medical records in prompts.

## Retention and deletion

Active-account data is retained while the account is used. Account deletion removes active records and uploaded photo files. Limited transaction, fraud-prevention, security, moderation, tax, and legal records may be retained where reasonably necessary. By default, local encrypted-backup rotation keeps the 14 newest backups plus one weekly backup for up to eight weeks. Manually downloaded or separately copied backups remain under the control of the operator or person who copied them and require manual secure deletion. Content already copied or screenshotted by another user cannot be guaranteed removable.

## Security

Controls include HTTPS in hosted use, password hashing, CSRF protection, secure sessions, rate limiting, audit logs, encrypted secrets and photos, and encrypted backups. No system is perfectly secure.

## User choices and privacy requests

Users may update profile data, export records, disable optional online AI, block users, report content, revoke sessions, and delete accounts. Depending on applicable law, users may also request access, correction, deletion, or a portable copy and may opt out of covered sale, targeted advertising, or certain profiling. FormForge does not sell personal information or use health or fitness data for targeted advertising. Identity verification may be required. Privacy requests go to the contact above.

## Texas privacy notice

Texas residents may have rights under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act when it applies. FormForge provides access, correction, export, and deletion tools even when a small-business exemption may apply. FormForge must not sell sensitive data without legally sufficient consent and the current product policy is not to sell personal information at all.

## Children

FormForge is not directed to children. Public accounts are limited to users age 18 or older, and the operator does not knowingly collect data from younger users.
