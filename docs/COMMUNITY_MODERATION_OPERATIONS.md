# Community Moderation Operations

## Implemented in FormForge 1.8.0

- Terms, Privacy Notice, Community Standards, and age confirmation before posting.
- Separate report and block actions.
- Reports for nudges, shared workouts, and user accounts.
- Immediate hiding of content for the reporting user while the report is open.
- Mutual content suppression after a block.
- Automated rejection of obvious abuse, threats, sexual exploitation language, hateful slurs, self-harm encouragement, and multi-link spam.
- Nudge rate limiting.
- Administrator queue showing report details and the reported content summary.
- Actions to dismiss, remove content, warn, suspend for 7 or 30 days, or ban community access.
- Moderation audit records and visible restriction reasons/end times.
- Community restrictions that leave private fitness tools available.

## Human review procedure

1. Review open reports promptly and prioritize threats, exploitation, self-harm encouragement, and privacy disclosures.
2. Read the reported content and reporter context; do not decide solely from the category label.
3. Preserve evidence before removal when a threat, fraud, or legal request may require it.
4. Choose the least severe action that protects users, while escalating repeat or severe conduct.
5. Enter a factual internal reason without unnecessary sensitive information.
6. Contact emergency services only when legally appropriate and when enough reliable information exists; the app itself is not an emergency service.
7. Document appeals and reversals outside the app until a dedicated appeal workflow is built.

## Known limits

- The text filter is intentionally narrow and cannot understand all context, coded language, images, or off-platform behavior.
- No third-party moderation vendor is connected.
- No automatic moderator email/push alert is connected.
- “Warn” creates an audit action but does not yet send a dedicated in-app warning inbox message.
- Appeals are handled through the published moderation email.
