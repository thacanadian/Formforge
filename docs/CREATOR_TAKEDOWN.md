# Creator Profile Takedown and Correction Process

FormForge profiles labeled **Editorial** are independent summaries and must not imply endorsement, partnership, approval, identity cloning, or authorship by the named creator.

## Submitting a request

A creator, rights holder, or authorized representative can open **Coaching Team → Creator takedown request** and provide the profile, requester name, contact information, detailed reason, and optional evidence URL. The public endpoint records the request even when the requester does not have a FormForge account.

## Administrator review

1. Acknowledge the request through the supplied contact information.
2. Confirm the requester’s relationship to the creator or protected material when reasonably necessary.
3. Set the request to `reviewing`.
4. Inspect the profile name, image/metadata, source summaries, quotes, links, licensing records, and marketing claims.
5. Immediately remove clearly unauthorized exact quotes, copied text, private material, cloned voice assets, or false Official labels.
6. Resolve by correcting or removing the profile, or reject with a documented reason when the claim is unsupported.

Marking a request `resolved` automatically removes a custom profile and removes it from user blends. Built-in editorial profiles require an application update or administrator removal of related sources; operators distributing FormForge should maintain an update process for these cases.

## Response target

This local software cannot enforce a universal legal deadline. A commercial operator should publish a working takedown contact, promptly acknowledge credible requests, preserve the audit trail, and obtain legal advice for disputed rights claims.
