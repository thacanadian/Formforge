# Terms, Plans, and Creator Rights

The in-app terms summary is stored in the application and versioned. Users can review and accept it under **Security**. Acceptance records the user, terms version, and time.

## Plan controls

The Free tier includes the local application and offline coach. The Pro tier permits online AI within administrator-defined daily limits. FormForge 1.4 provides entitlement and usage controls but does not process payments, subscriptions, refunds, taxes, or app-store purchases. A commercial deployment must integrate its own billing provider and keep billing credentials outside the client.

## Editorial profiles

Editorial creator profiles are not endorsements or official programs. They must not claim that a creator wrote an answer, personally coaches the user, approved FormForge, or licensed their identity. Descriptive summaries should use original language. Exact quotations require source verification. Official status requires documented permission.

## Distribution review

The bundled language is a product safeguard, not legal advice. Before public commercialization, obtain jurisdiction-specific review covering right of publicity, trademark, copyright, privacy, biometric/health data, medical disclaimers, consumer subscriptions, auto-renewal, refund policy, tax, and platform-store rules.
