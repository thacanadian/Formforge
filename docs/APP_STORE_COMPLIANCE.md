# App Store and Google Play Compliance Notes

## Built into FormForge 1.8.0

- Public privacy, terms, subscription, health/AI, community, security, and deletion pages.
- In-app links to legal pages.
- Versioned acceptance records and renewed-consent capability.
- Adult age confirmation.
- In-app and external account deletion.
- Report, block, filter, moderation, suspension, and ban controls for community content.
- Optional online AI with offline fallback and disclosure.

## Still required before store launch

- Native iOS and Android packaging with meaningful device functionality.
- StoreKit and Google Play Billing subscriptions.
- Server-side entitlement validation, store server notifications, restore purchases, cancellation links, and billing-state handling.
- Native privacy permission descriptions, HealthKit/Health Connect disclosures, and physical-device tests.
- Apple App Privacy, Google Data Safety, Google Health Apps, content-rating, export-compliance, and reviewer-access forms.
- Real production operator/contact details and final attorney-reviewed pages.
- A production privacy map of every enabled processor and SDK.
- A support process, moderation response schedule, and incident-response process.

The source must not claim store approval, HIPAA compliance, medical certification, or complete legal compliance merely because these controls exist.
